feat(options): add a Usage page with your plan and AI quota, and open settings on Preference

Settings now has a Usage page, first in the sidebar. It shows your account and plan, your Built-in AI quota and your AI subtitles quota in one place. Pro and Ultra subscribers get a Manage subscription button. A free account gets an Upgrade button, and someone not logged in gets a Log in button with a note that logging in gives free AI quota every week. The quota sections on the API Providers and Video Subtitles pages moved here. Opening settings now lands on Preference instead of API Providers.
