feat(built-in-ai): run Built-in AI on Claude Haiku 5.5 instead of GPT-6 Luna

New installs start every Built-in AI feature on Claude Haiku 5.5. When you update, every feature, selection toolbar action, language detection and Translation Hub card that runs on GPT-6 Luna moves to Claude Haiku 5.5, including ones you picked yourself. A Translation Hub card for GPT-6 Luna merges into an existing Claude Haiku 5.5 card. Features on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, GPT-6.2 Sol, Claude Sonnet 5.5 or your own providers stay where they are. A reasoning level you set for GPT-6 Luna does not carry over: Claude Haiku 5.5 starts on its own default, and switching back to GPT-6 Luna brings back its level.
