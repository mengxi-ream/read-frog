feat(options): add simple mode to turn off the features you don't use

A new Simple mode page under Advanced in the options has a switch for each feature: Custom AI Actions, Page Translation (and its Hover translation), Video Subtitles, Input Translation, Floating Button, Selection Toolbar (and its Word annotation, Selection translation with Note suggestions, and Speak), Context Menu, Translation Hub and Glossary. A feature you turn off is as if it weren't installed: it no longer shows on web pages and its shortcuts do nothing. Its settings are kept, so turning it back on brings everything back as it was. Its pages leave the options sidebar, except the Custom AI Actions page and Page Translation's pages. Page Translation's pages stay because selection translation, input translation and the Translation Hub use its prompts, translation queue and cache. The popup has a new Simple mode button at the top that opens the same switches. With Page Translation off, the popup hides its page translation controls, and the context menu has no item to translate the page. A click on the floating button, when it is set to translate the page, then shows a message beside it that Page Translation is turned off in Simple mode, with a button that goes to its switch. A message beside a button near the edge of the page, such as the floating button's, no longer shrinks to a narrow column. With Custom AI Actions off, the selection toolbar, its ⋮ menu and the context menu no longer show AI actions, and on the Shortcuts page their keys show a Turn on link. The ⋮ button in the selection toolbar now only shows when its menu has something in it. The Enable switches at the top of the feature pages are gone, because Simple mode has them now, and a link to turn a feature on, such as on the Shortcuts page, goes to its switch there. The hide menus of the selection toolbar and the floating button now also name Options → Simple mode as a place to turn them back on. Turning off the whole selection toolbar, here or by hiding it on all sites, now also turns off its shortcuts; hiding it until reload or on one site keeps them. The popup no longer has its own Discord and blog buttons: What's New is now the first item under More, with a dot when there is a post you haven't read, and Discord is still under More.
