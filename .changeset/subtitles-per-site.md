feat(subtitles): choose per site whether video subtitles are off, started from the button, or started automatically. "Enable video subtitles" now opens one menu per site (YouTube, X, Netflix, Bilibili, Udemy, edX) in place of the global switch and the separate "Translate on open" page, and the player's subtitle settings can switch auto-start for the site you are on. Existing settings carry over: subtitles switched off become off on every site, and sites that translated on open become automatic
